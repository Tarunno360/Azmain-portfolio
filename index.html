Research Experience

Independent research with Nabil Walid Rafat · Mar 2026 – Present

CisRAG: Confidence-Aware Agentic Retrieval for Cis-Regulatory QA

  • Developed the methodology, architecture, implementation, baseline comparisons, and end-to-end RAG pipeline.
  • Coordinates a fine-tuned dual-head Omni-DNA promoter/enhancer classifier, a FAISS knowledge base (EPDnew, ENCODE), and FIMO motif scanning against JASPAR CORE.
  • Calibrated confidence and similarity thresholds route evidence retrieval, corroborate sources, and enable abstention when evidence is insufficient.
  • Evaluated 1,680 questions across 4 genomic question types against 7 baseline configurations using Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct and Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct: 92.8–94.8% classification accuracy versus 48.1–60.0% for LLM-only baselines; 99.5% gene identification; 98.9–99.6% correct abstention on out-of-knowledge-base genes; approximately 23% fewer source invocations than exhaustive retrieval at comparable accuracy.
